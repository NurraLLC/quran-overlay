Your privacy in Quran Reader

Effective October 2, 2026 · Operated by Nurra LLC

You can read without an account or microphone. This policy covers Quran Reader at nurra.org/quran-reader, including its streaming overlay. Contact ashfaq@nurra.org about privacy, access, correction or deletion.

What happens when you use it

  • Reading: our server receives the verses you request and normal connection information such as your IP address. Your last reading position, language and today's recited-ayah list are saved in this browser's local storage.
  • Listening: only after your separate agreement and browser microphone permission, audio passes through our server to Soniox for real-time speech recognition. Nurra does not record or store audio or transcripts on disk in the public service. Recognised words are held temporarily in your active session to follow your recitation.
  • Finding an ayah: typed requests and recognised words may be sent with a bounded set of candidates to OpenRouter and TypeSafe. These services help select an existing ayah; they do not write scripture. Recitation and requests can reveal religious beliefs, which are sensitive information. Do not include private information about yourself or anyone else in a request.
  • Your own Soniox key (optional): if you enter a key in the stream controls, it is saved in this browser until you remove it or clear site data. Each listening stream sends it over an encrypted connection to Nurra's server to connect to Soniox. It is held temporarily in server memory and is not saved to server disk. Remove affects future streams; Stop listening closes the current one.
  • Fair use: a signed anonymous cookie identifies your browser. We keep listening duration, time reservations, IP addresses and abuse counters to share the budget fairly and prevent misuse. These are not public profiles.
  • Support: when payments are available, Stripe handles checkout and payment details. Our ledger stores payment identifiers, amounts, fees and accounting times. Nurra does not receive your full card number. We do not publish names from contributions to Nurra's shared listening pool.
  • Optional charity scene: separately from contributions to Nurra, a stream creator can enter donor names, messages and amounts to announce on their own stream. These entries are saved with the scene settings. Only enter information you have permission to share; an empty name is shown as anonymous.
  • OBS: people with your private overlay link can access your chosen reading display and saved charity scene data, including entered names, messages and amounts even when amounts are hidden on screen. Treat that link as private and rotate it in the controls if you share it accidentally. Your stream platform has its own privacy practices.

Why we process this information

We process reading requests to provide the service you ask for, voice and recognised words with your explicit agreement, and limited operational records to protect the service and account for its costs. Financial records may also be needed for legal obligations. We do not sell your information, build advertising profiles, or use your recitation to train our own AI models.

Services involved

Our reader server is hosted in New York, USA. Website delivery and providers may process information outside your country. Their privacy policies describe their own processing and international-transfer arrangements:

  • Soniox: real-time speech recognition.
  • OpenRouter and its selected TypeSafe provider: bounded matching and search decisions. Requests disable provider fallback and request no data collection; this is not a promise that all technical metadata is absent.
  • DigitalOcean: reader hosting; OpenAI Sites and Cloudflare: website delivery.
  • Stripe: optional payment processing.
  • Quran Foundation: public Quran content, translations and font resources. We use its content APIs, not its user accounts, bookmarks or QuranReflect personal data. Quran Reader is an independent Nurra product, not an official Quran Foundation, Quran.com or QuranReflect app.

Storage and controls

Stop listening to close the microphone and recognition stream. Closing the page also ends listening. Reloading the page asks for voice agreement again. You can revoke microphone permission in browser settings. Reading does not require it.

Clear this site's browser data to remove saved reading position and the anonymous cookie. This does not erase server accounting records. Temporary reader sessions are removed after they have no open controls or listening stream and have been idle for about 30 minutes. Inactive abuse counters are removed after 30 days. Listening and financial ledger entries currently remain for accounting and abuse protection; request review or deletion by email. We may retain records required for financial obligations or ongoing security investigations.

We use HTTPS, restricted server access and server-side provider credentials. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Hosting and payment providers may keep separate operational records under their policies.

Questions and changes

You can request access, correction, deletion or restriction of information associated with your use at ashfaq@nurra.org. Please do not email audio, passwords, card details or private overlay links. Depending on where you live, you may also have a right to complain to your local data-protection authority. We will explain any information needed to identify your anonymous session.

This service is not directed at children under 13. Parents should supervise children's use and contact us if a child has sent personal information. Material changes will be noted here with an updated effective date; changes to voice processing will require fresh agreement.
