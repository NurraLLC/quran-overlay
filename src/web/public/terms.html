Quran Reader terms of use

Effective September 29, 2026 · Nurra LLC

Quran Reader is a free reading and recitation-following tool operated by Nurra LLC. These terms explain the service offered at nurra.org/quran-reader. Questions or payment problems: ashfaq@nurra.org.

Reading and listening

No reader account or purchase is required. Listening depends on available community funding, provider availability and fair-use limits. It can pause when funding is exhausted or limits are reached. Reading and manual navigation do not require speech recognition. We may maintain, change or suspend features when needed to operate and protect the service.

Use it with care

Automatic following can select the wrong location or miss words. Check the displayed ayah, especially before broadcasting it. The tool is not a tajwid assessment, religious ruling or substitute for a qualified teacher. It displays existing Quran text and translations; it does not generate scripture or recitation.

Do not bypass usage limits, automate wasteful listening, attack the service, collect others' private information, or use content unlawfully or in ways that misrepresent the Quran. Misuse may lead to a temporary or longer restriction.

Community support

When enabled, contributions are voluntary, one-time payments to Nurra LLC for Quran Reader's running costs, including recognition, hosting, AI and payment fees. They do not buy personal listening hours or a subscription and are not represented as tax-deductible charitable donations. Hour equivalents are estimates for the shared budget, not guaranteed hours delivered. Actual recitation time, recorded costs and funds set aside are shown separately.

For an accidental or disputed payment, contact us promptly with your payment receipt reference. We will review the request; these terms do not limit rights you have under applicable law. Never email your card details. Fundraisers run by streamers for other organizations are separate and use those organizations' own payment arrangements.

Content and streaming

Quran content and display resources come through Quran Foundation; the English translation is Saheeh International. The app's source-code license does not grant rights to redistribute third-party datasets or assets. Respect their source-specific terms. For streams using this content, credit Quran Foundation in a reasonably accessible place, such as your video description, alongside any Nurra credit.

You control what you broadcast and who receives your overlay link. Do not broadcast another person's voice without their permission. Follow your streaming platform's rules.

Privacy and updates

The reader privacy policy describes data use and choices. Microphone processing requires separate agreement. We may update these terms by publishing a revised effective date. Nothing here removes protections that applicable law gives you.
